Base versus downside

Each row prints three published figures for the selected borrower: the base value, the downside value, and the pipeline's delta envelope with the label the pipeline published beside it. A delta of exactly zero prints that published label, invariant by construction, and the browser never subtracts: every delta is computed in the pipeline and committed with the payload.

Rate shock

The shocked schedule prints its own published figures: shocked lender IRR, shocked total interest, the IRR delta, and the sensitivity per 100 bp. Both delta figures carry unit bp in the value, printed from the committed envelope, and a shock that publishes no figure shows its stated reason instead.
