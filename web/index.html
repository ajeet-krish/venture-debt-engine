How figures reach this page

The pipeline commits canonical payloads, the site copies them at build time, and the loader validates their shape before a single field is read. Formatting places decimal points, groups digits, and renders dates. Any sum, ratio, or difference in the browser would breach that boundary, so the rule is stated here rather than assumed.
